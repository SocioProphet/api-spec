# ADR-0002: Identity as prime plane

## Decision
Identity is not an auxiliary concern. It is the transversal substrate beneath and across transport, placement, lifecycle, artifact handling, and execution.

## Normative doctrine
- No action without identity.
- No delegation without capability.
- No cross-boundary acceptance without attestation.
- No persistence without retention owner.
- No irreversible actuation without stronger identity, evidence, and approval requirements.
- No rebirth without authority continuity or explicit reissuance.

## Consequence
Every workflow, run, artifact, operator invocation, and cache entry must be attributable to a principal and bounded by capability and revocation.
