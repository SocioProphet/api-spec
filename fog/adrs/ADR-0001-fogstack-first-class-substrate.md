# ADR-0001: FogStack as a first-class Prophet substrate

## Decision
We adopt FogStack as the operator/control substrate for Prophet platform edge/fog services.

## Why
FogStack already defines:
- interface-neutral adapter strategy
- canonical dashboard surfaces
- artifact/release handling
- deployment profiles
- observability and planning lanes

Helix contributes structural normalization and schema linkage.
Socrates contributes unary/binary/implicit knowledge induction.
Identity plane contributes trust, capability, delegation, attestation,
and revocation semantics.

## Consequence
All operational state, topology, telemetry, promotion, and policy actions
must be represented as canonical contracts and evidence-bearing claims.
